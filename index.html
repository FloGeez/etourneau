iii. Le mouvement

etourneau/starling

Everything the bird does: it raises its wing, turns its head, walks, shoots, flaps and glides, takes off and lands. The same drawing, cut into parts that turn around their pivots (the wing around the shoulder, the head, the legs), with no second drawing: geometry and a little maths, the wing beat in an SVG, one animation loop. No dependency.

uses
nothing
  • Every gesture, in slow motion

iv. La nuée

etourneau/murmuration

Hundreds of small starlings that land on points and draw shapes: here, the bird itself. Point at it: they take off, then land on their place again. The engine is open source, @flogeez/murmuration (MIT, a plain bird); this layer gives it the starling, the purple and the brand's shapes.

uses
etourneau/starling, @flogeez/murmuration

  • The open-source engine
